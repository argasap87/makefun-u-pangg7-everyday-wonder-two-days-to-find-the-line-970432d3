One sentence on a small moment of wonder in everyday life, entered in
[Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3)
on make.fun.

A gnat stood on my thumb and walked the whorl of my fingerprint — and I
realised I have carried that tiny landscape for forty years without once
reading it.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry sentence to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(`/output/entry.txt` by default).
